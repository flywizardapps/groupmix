The GroupMix App

What the app stores

In GroupMix, you create courses and add people: names and, if you like, gender and an assessment of their level. In addition, the app stores rules about who should work together or apart, and saved groupings from the last 14 days. If you use Random Pick, the app remembers when each person last had a turn, so that everyone gets one. This isn’t included in backups.

Where the data stays

  • All data is stored only on your device and encrypted by iOS and iPadOS Data Protection while the device is locked.
  • The app has no internet connection, no account, and no cloud sync. The data isn’t included in the device’s iCloud or computer backup either.
  • There is no tracking, no advertising, and no third-party analytics or crash reporting.
  • We, as the provider, have no access to the data in your app.

Sharing and backups

  • Sharing groups: When you share a grouping as an image or text, it contains only the groups with names, never level, gender, or rules. You decide where to share it. The terms of the recipient or service apply.
  • Saving an image to Photos: If you choose “Save Image” when sharing, iOS asks once whether GroupMix may add images to your Photos. GroupMix can’t see or read your photos. If iCloud Photos is turned on, the image syncs with iCloud like any other photo.
  • Backups: A backup is only created when you start it yourself. The file is encrypted with a password that you set and that isn’t stored anywhere. You decide where to keep the file, for example in Files or via AirDrop.

Screen mirroring

The app detects whether the screen is being mirrored or recorded and then hides level, gender, and rules. No data is collected or stored in the process.

Apple

The app is distributed through Apple’s App Store. Apple’s privacy policy applies to downloading, installing, and updating it. If you agree in your device settings to share analytics with app developers, Apple provides us with anonymized crash reports. These contain no content from the app. If you join a test through TestFlight, Apple shares the name and email address you provided for it, as well as your feedback, with us.

Use at school

If you use GroupMix for teaching, you or your school are responsible for your students’ data, not us. Follow the rules of your school and region for using personal devices. Keep personal data only as long as necessary. GroupMix helps with “Clean Up” at the end of a term.
