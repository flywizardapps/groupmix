Guide

How to make groups and pick people at random with GroupMix: from your first course to backups and cleaning up at the end of a term.

Quick Start: Your First Grouping in Two Minutes

  1. Create a course: In the course list, tap “+”, enter the name, and tap the checkmark. Or start right away with “Try with Sample Course”. Read more
  2. Add people: In the course, tap “+”, choose “Paste Name List”, paste the list (one name per line), and tap the checkmark. Read more
  3. Check attendance: “Present Today” shows who is there. Read more
  4. Make groups: Tap “Make Groups” at the bottom, choose the group size, and tap “Create Groups” at the bottom. Read more
  5. Save and show: Tap “Done” at the top right to save the grouping, or tap “Share” at the bottom right. Read more

Getting Ready

You set up courses, people, and rules once. After that, making groups during a lesson is quick.

Getting Started

Sample course

When you first open GroupMix, it shows a short introduction. “Try with Sample Course” gives you a course with fictional people where you can safely try everything out. You delete it like any other course. You’ll find the introduction later in Settings.

Create a course

  1. In the course list, tap “+” at the top.
  2. Enter the name, for example 8b, and optionally a “Term” (for example 2026/27 or a semester like Fall 2026) and a “Description” (for example Mathematics).
  3. Tap the checkmark.

The course list is organized by term. At the end of a term, GroupMix helps you clean up.

Edit and Delete a Course

Touch and hold a course in the course list, or tap “…” at the top of the course. There you’ll find “Edit”, “Copy for New Term”, and “Delete”.

  • Deleting also removes all people, rules, and groupings in the course.
  • Copy for New Term copies the people and rules, but no groupings. 8b becomes 9b, and the old course stays unchanged.

Adding People

In the course, tap “+” at the top. You can add a person, paste a name list, or import a CSV file.

Add a person

  1. Tap “+” and choose “Add Person”.
  2. Enter the name, for example Hedwig L., and optionally set gender and level.
  3. Tap the checkmark. “Save and Add Another Person” lets you add several people in a row.

Paste a name list

  1. Copy the list, for example from an email, Excel, or Numbers.
  2. In the course, tap “+”, choose “Paste Name List”, and paste the list.
  3. Check the preview and tap the checkmark.

One name per line. Gender and level can follow after a semicolon:

Hedwig L.;f;4
Konrad M.;m
Ottokar T.
  • Gender: m, f, x. Empty means not specified.
  • Level: 1 (lower) to 5 (higher). Empty means medium.

Columns copied from Excel or Numbers work too.

Import from a CSV file

  1. In the course, tap “+” and choose “Import from File”.
  2. Tap “Choose CSV File…” and select the file.
  3. Check the preview and tap the checkmark.

Export a spreadsheet from Excel or Numbers as CSV first. GroupMix doesn’t read other formats such as .xlsx.

Tip

The easiest way is the template: “Share Template …” gives you a CSV file that you fill in with Numbers or Excel and export as CSV again.

CSV file format

The first row contains the column titles, followed by one person per row:

First Name;Last Name;Gender;Level
Hedwig;Lange;f;4
Konrad;Maurer;m;
  • “Name” or “First Name” and “Last Name”: Hedwig and Lange become Hedwig L.
  • “Gender” and “Level” (optional): values as in the name list.

The order of the columns doesn’t matter, and additional columns are ignored. GroupMix also recognizes German column titles (such as Vorname, Nachname, Geschlecht, Niveau).

Gender and Level

Both are optional and only matter when making groups.

  • Gender: “m”, “f”, “x”, or “n/a” (not specified, the default). It only has an effect if you turn on “Balance Gender”.
  • Level: Your assessment of the person compared to the course, in five steps from “Low” to “High” (default: “Medium”). It only has an effect with “Mixed Levels” and “Similar Levels”.

Both are hidden in the list of people. Use “…” and “Show Level and Gender” to show them.

Keep levels up to date

Via “…” and “Edit Levels”, you change the levels of everyone in one list. To keep them current, GroupMix asks “Are the levels still right?” from time to time. You choose how often in Settings.

Privacy

Level and gender never appear in a shared image or text and are hidden while you mirror your screen.

Edit and Delete People

Touch and hold a person, or tap “Edit” at the top of the course. There you’ll find “Edit” and “Delete”. A deleted person is also removed from their rules.

Rules: Together or Apart

With rules, you decide who should work together or apart.

  1. On the “Grouping” screen, tap “Rules”.
  2. Tap “+” at the top.
  3. Choose “Apart” or “Together” and check at least two people.
  4. Tap the checkmark.

You can also create rules in a person’s form or right from the result.

Use the switch in each row to turn a rule off for a while. To edit or delete a rule, touch and hold it.

If two rules contradict each other, GroupMix shows a warning symbol. “Apart” then takes precedence over “Together”.

App Language

GroupMix is available in English, German, French, Spanish, Dutch, Danish, Swedish, and Norwegian and follows your device’s language. You can also choose a different language for GroupMix, for example German for a German class:

  1. In GroupMix Settings, tap “Language”. The iOS Settings open.
  2. Under “Language”, choose the language you want.
  3. Switch back to GroupMix.

If iOS doesn’t offer a choice there, only one language is set up on your device. Then add a second one in the iOS Settings under “General” › “Language & Region”, and keep your current one as the device language.

Good to know

Anything you entered yourself, such as course and people’s names, stays as it is. GroupMix recognizes name lists and CSV files in all offered languages. If the app is set to Spanish, “m” means female (“mujer”). In languages other than English and German, Settings offer “Report a Translation Issue”.

Settings

Tap the gear at the top of the course list. On iPad with the sidebar, “Settings” is in the “…” menu of the course list.

  • Backup: create and import a backup.
  • Reminders: for backups, levels, and cleaning up at the end of a term. You can turn each one off and, for backups and levels, choose how often GroupMix reminds you.
  • App Language: see App Language.
  • Help: introduction and guide.

Multiple Windows on iPad

On iPad, you can use GroupMix in several windows, for example two courses side by side.

  • Course in a new window: In the course list, touch and hold a course and choose “Open in New Window”.
  • New window: In the menu bar, choose File > “New Window” (⌥⌘N).

All windows show the same data, and changes appear everywhere right away. When moving people, you can also drag names from one window to another.

In Class

From attendance to grouping to Random Pick: what you need during the lesson.

Attendance

Under “Present Today”, use the switch next to each person to set whether they’re there. Absent people are left out when making groups and in Random Pick. On every new day, everyone is marked present again.

Making Groups

  1. In the course, tap “Make Groups” at the bottom. “Groups” is selected at the top. Next to it, you’ll find “Manual” and “Jigsaw”.
  2. Choose the group size and composition. GroupMix remembers the settings for each course.
  3. Tap “Create Groups” at the bottom. “Regroup” gives you a different split.

Group size

Choose “Number of Groups” or “People per Group”. Below it, you see the outcome. If the split doesn’t work out evenly, choose between “Prefer Smaller Groups” and “Prefer Larger Groups”. Both show the concrete result. With “People per Group”, nobody works alone: If one person is left over when making pairs, there’s a group of three.

Composition

  • “Random”: Everyone is assigned at random.
  • “Mixed Levels”: Each group gets people with higher and lower levels and is about equally strong on average.
  • “Similar Levels”: People with similar levels are grouped together.

You can also turn on:

  • “Balance Gender”: Male and female are distributed across the groups as evenly as possible.
  • “Avoid Repeats”: People who were together in the last three saved groupings are kept apart where possible. Pinned groupings such as table groups count too. This has the clearest effect with “Random”.

Group size always takes precedence, then rules, then composition. GroupMix tells you in the result if something couldn’t be met.

Setting Groups Yourself

If the groups already exist, for example as table groups, enter them yourself.

  1. In the course, tap “Make Groups” at the bottom and choose “Manual” at the top.
  2. Tap the people in the first group. “New Group” starts the next one.
  3. Tap “Continue” at the bottom, then “Done” in the result.

To change a group, tap it. If you go back, your entries are kept until you delete them with “Discard Entries”.

Groups you need for longer, you pin.

Jigsaw

In a jigsaw, everyone gets a topic in their home group. In expert groups, everyone with the same topic works together, then they explain it in their home group.

  1. In the course, tap “Make Groups” at the bottom and choose “Jigsaw” at the top.
  2. Set the number of topics and choose a suggestion under “Groups”.
  3. Tap “Create Groups” at the bottom.

In the result, switch between “Home Groups” and “Expert Groups” at the top. Topics are called A, B, C. With “…” and “Name Topics…”, you give them names. GroupMix splits expert groups with more than five people. Sharing creates two images.

Good to know

If a home group has nobody present for a topic, GroupMix says so above the cards and suggests who from another group can help out. “Switch” moves that person.

With “Mixed Levels”, both home groups and expert groups are mixed. This is recommended for a jigsaw.

Notices After Making Groups

If GroupMix couldn’t fulfill a rule, the top of the result says, for example, “1 rule not met”. Which rule it is only appears when you tap it. That way no one reads along in passing. The notice is hidden during screen mirroring.

Adjusting the Result

More features are in the “…” menu at the top right of the result, for example “Move People”, “Add Person”, “Create Rule”, roles and topics, and picking.

Move people

  1. Tap “…” at the top and choose “Move People”.
  2. Touch and hold a name and drag it to another group.
  3. Tap “Stop”.

With VoiceOver, choose the action “Move to Group …” on a name.

Undo, save, discard

  • The arrow at the bottom left undoes the last change, including “Regroup”.
  • “Done” saves the grouping with your adjustments.
  • “Back” discards a result that hasn’t been saved yet.

When GroupMix also saves automatically is described under Saving Groupings.

Someone Arrives Late

  1. In the result, tap “…” at the top and choose “Add Person”.
  2. Tap the person.
  3. Choose the group. The person is then marked present.

The list shows everyone in the course who is missing from the grouping. New people are added in the course.

Create a Rule from the Result

If you notice during the lesson that certain people should work apart or together in the future, create the rule right here.

  1. Tap “…” at the top and choose “Create Rule”.
  2. Tap at least two names, then tap “Continue”.
  3. Choose “Apart” or “Together” and tap the checkmark.

The rule applies to future groupings. “Create Rule” isn’t available while you mirror your screen.

Roles and Topics

Assign roles

  1. In the result, tap “…” at the top and choose “Assign Roles…”.
  2. Check the roles and tap the checkmark.

Everyone gets a random role, if possible a different one than in recent groupings. It appears on the card below the name. Above the cards, switch to “Roles” to see each role’s goal and tips; tap one to show it full screen.

If a group has fewer people than roles, it doesn’t get all of them. Use the lock to choose roles that every group gets. Add your own roles with “+” at the bottom; to adjust a standard role, edit a copy.

Assign topics

  1. In the result, tap “…” at the top and choose “Assign Topics…”.
  2. Enter the topics, one per field, and tap the checkmark.

Each group gets a random topic. It appears at the top of the card.

Afterwards, the “…” menu shows “Roles” and “Topics”, with “Reassign” and “Remove”. If roles are assigned, sharing creates a second image with the roles.

Show and Share on the Projector

To show the grouping on the projector, mirror your screen. What stays hidden is described under Mirroring Your Screen.

Share

  1. In the result, tap “Share” at the bottom right and choose “Share as Image” or “Share as Text”.
  2. Choose a destination, for example AirDrop, “Save to Files”, or “Save Image” for the Photos app.

The image shows the course name and the group cards with the names. The text has one line per group:

Group 1: Hedwig L., Konrad M., Ottokar T.

Who Presents?

After group work, GroupMix picks at random who presents each group’s results and in which order the groups take their turn. Since anyone can be picked, each group makes sure that everyone can present its results. You find both in the “…” menu, also in saved groupings.

Pick one per group

Tap “…” at the top and choose “Pick One per Group”. In each group, one name is marked in bold. “Pick Again” picks anew in all groups, “Stop” removes the marks.

Pick a group

  1. Tap “…” at the top and choose “Pick a Group”. The picked group shows its position, for example “1.”.
  2. Tap “Pick a Person” in the notice to pick who presents for this group.
  3. Tap “Next Group” for the next one. “Stop” ends the order and removes all marks.

People who are absent are skipped. GroupMix remembers where you are: If you open the grouping again in the next lesson, just continue with “Next Group”.

Random Pick

Random Pick decides whose turn it is, for example who presents the results of partner work. Only people who are present are picked.

  1. In the course, tap the die at the bottom left.
  2. Tap “Pick”. Tap again for the next person.

Everyone once

By default, everyone gets one turn before a new round starts. The count carries over across lessons, and people who are absent get their turn later. In the “…” menu, you can turn off “Avoid Repeats” or start over with “Reset Count”.

Picked someone by accident? The “Undo” arrow at the bottom left puts the person back.

Groups Over Several Lessons

If groups stay together over several lessons, you reopen the saved grouping and adjust it if needed.

Saving Groupings

GroupMix saves a grouping when you tap “Done” in the result, share it, or pick from it. It also saves automatically when you mirror your screen or leave the app while the result is open. “Back” discards a result that hasn’t been saved yet.

Open the Last Grouping

At the very top of the course, under “Groupings”, “Last Grouping” opens the most recently saved grouping, for example in the next lesson. People who aren’t here today appear dimmed on the cards. If group picking is still in progress, the course shows it.

Adjust a Saved Grouping

You can also change a saved grouping, for example when someone is missing or joins in the second lesson. The “…” menu offers “Move People”, “Add Person”, and “Create Rule”, used just like in the result. When moving people, “Stop” saves your changes.

Good to know

If you adjust a grouping from an earlier day, a new grouping for today is created. The old one stays unchanged in the history.

Pinning Groupings

Pin fixed groups such as table groups with a name. Pinned groupings stay in the course until you delete them and appear under “Groupings”.

  1. In a grouping, tap “…” at the top and choose “Pin…”.
  2. Enter a name, for example Table Groups, and tap “Pin”.

When you change a pinned grouping, GroupMix asks when saving:

  • “Save for Today Only”: The change applies only today, for example when some people are missing. The pinned grouping stays as it is.
  • “Change … Permanently”: The pinned grouping itself changes.

You’ll find “Rename…” and “Delete” in the “…” menu of the pinned grouping.

History

In the course, tap “History” next to “Groupings”. Tap a grouping to view, share, or adjust it. To delete it, touch and hold it.

GroupMix keeps groupings for 14 days; older ones are deleted automatically. The newest one is always kept.

Privacy in Class

Level, gender, and rules are meant for your eyes only. To keep them from showing up in front of the class by accident, GroupMix protects them automatically.

Mirroring Your Screen

When you mirror your screen via AirPlay or cable, or record it, GroupMix detects this automatically. There’s nothing to set up. While mirroring:

  • level, gender, and rules are hidden everywhere, including in forms and notices,
  • group cards and picked names get larger,
  • the screen doesn’t lock while groups or Random Pick are showing.

These details also stay hidden in the app switcher as soon as you leave GroupMix.

GroupMix can’t detect a document camera pointed at the device.

What Is Never Shared

Privacy

Image and text contain only names. Level, gender, and rules never appear in them. How to share is described under Show and Share on the Projector.

Backups and End of Term

Backup

GroupMix stores everything encrypted and only on your device. The data is not included in the iCloud or computer backup. So back it up yourself. A backup is also how you move to another device.

Create a backup

  1. In Settings, choose “Create Backup”.
  2. Enter a password with at least 8 characters twice and tap the checkmark.
  3. Share the file, for example save it in Files or send it via AirDrop.
Important

The password isn’t stored anywhere. Without it, a backup can’t be restored.

The backup contains all courses with their people, rules, history, and pinned groupings, as well as your own roles. It doesn’t include who has had a turn in Random Pick.

Import a backup

  1. Tap the backup file, for example in Files, or choose “Import Backup” in Settings.
  2. Enter the password.
  3. If there are already courses on the device, choose: “Add to Existing” adds the courses from the backup, “Replace” deletes the existing courses and keeps those from the backup.
  4. Tap the checkmark.

If your last backup is a while ago, GroupMix reminds you at the top of the course list (by default every month).

Switching Devices

Create a backup on the old device, send the file to the new device via AirDrop or Files, and open it there.

End of a Term

You should keep personal data only as long as necessary. When a term is over, for example a school year from 1 August or a semester, GroupMix reminds you at the top of the course list.

  1. Tap “Clean Up” in the notice.
  2. Check the new term. With “Increment Names”, 8b automatically becomes 9b.
  3. Check the courses you’re carrying over and move them. People, rules, and history are kept.
  4. Delete the remaining courses.
Good to know

Deleted courses can only be restored from a backup. If you’re unsure, create a backup first.

To copy a single course, use “Copy for New Term” (see Edit and Delete a Course).

FAQ

Why is “Add Person” dimmed?

Then no one is missing from the grouping. More under Someone Arrives Late.

Why is “Make Groups” dimmed?

Then no one is present. Use the “Mark All Present” button above the list of people. More under Attendance.

What do the students see on the projector?

Only names and groups. While you mirror your screen, GroupMix automatically hides level, gender, and rules. More under Mirroring Your Screen.

Do level or gender appear in the shared image?

No. Image and text contain only names. More under What Is Never Shared.

Is every grouping saved?

Every one you use: with “Done”, when sharing, picking, or mirroring, and when you leave the app. “Back” discards a result that hasn’t been saved yet. More under Saving Groupings.

How do I keep fixed table groups?

Enter them with “Manual” and pin them. More under Setting Groups Yourself and Pinning Groupings.

How long do groupings stay in the history?

14 days; the newest one is always kept. Pinned groupings stay until you delete them. More under History.

Can I use GroupMix in a different language?

Yes, independently of your device language. More under App Language.

Can I turn off the reminders?

Yes, each one individually in Settings under “Reminders”. More under Settings.

How do I get my data onto a new device?

With a backup. More under Switching Devices.
