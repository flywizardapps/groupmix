Frequently Asked Questions

Where is my data stored?

Only on your device, encrypted. GroupMix uses no cloud, no account, and no internet connection. The data isn’t included in the device’s iCloud or computer backup either.

If you lose the device or delete the app, the data is gone unless you created a backup (see the next question).

How do I back up my data or move it to another device?

In the course list, tap “…” and “Create Backup” to make an encrypted backup file, then import it on the other device with “Import Backup”. The password isn’t stored anywhere.

The steps are in the guide: Backups and Switching Devices.

What happens when I mirror my screen?

As soon as you mirror your screen via AirPlay or cable, or record it, GroupMix automatically hides level, gender, and rules. Group cards get larger, and the display stays on while the groups are shown. There’s nothing to set up. More in the guide under Showing and Sharing the Result.

How do I add a class list?

In a course, tap “+”: You can add people one by one, paste a name list, or import a CSV file.

The exact format is in the guide: Adding People.

What does the level mean?

The level is your assessment of where a person stands compared to the rest of the course, for example their current attainment in your subject. You choose one of five steps from “Low” to “High”. It’s optional.

With the level, you can make groups in two ways:

  • Mixed Levels: Each group gets people with higher and lower attainment and is about equally strong on average.
  • Similar Levels: People with similar attainment work together, for example on tasks of different difficulty.

The level is only used for making groups. It never appears in shared images or text and is hidden while you mirror your screen. More in the guide under Gender and level.

What do I do at the end of the school year?

When a term is over, the course list shows a notice at the top. With “Clean Up”, you carry courses over to the new term and delete the rest.

The steps are in the guide: End of a Term.

Can I use GroupMix at school?

GroupMix processes names and, if you enter them, assessments of your students’ attainment. Using personal devices for school work is subject to the data protection rules of your school and region. If in doubt, ask your school’s leadership or data protection officer.

How GroupMix handles data is described in the privacy policy.
