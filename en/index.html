Frequently Asked Questions

Where is my data stored?

Only on your device, encrypted. GroupMix uses no cloud, no account, and no internet connection. The data isn’t included in the device’s iCloud or computer backup either.

If you lose the device or delete the app, the data is gone unless you created a backup (see the next question).

How do I back up my data or move it to another device?

Create a backup

  1. In the course list, tap “…” at the top and choose “Create Backup”.
  2. Enter a password with at least 8 characters twice and tap the checkmark.
  3. Tap “Share” and save the file in Files or send it to your other device via AirDrop.

Import a backup

  1. Tap the backup file, for example in Files. Or tap “…” at the top of the course list and choose “Import Backup”.
  2. Enter the password.
  3. Choose “Add to Existing” to keep your current courses, or “Replace” to keep only the courses from the backup. Then tap the checkmark.

The password isn’t stored anywhere. Without it, a backup can’t be restored.

Backup reminder

If your last backup was more than 30 days ago, GroupMix shows a notice at the top of the course list. “Remind Me Later” hides it for 7 days. To turn the reminder off completely, tap “…” in the course list and switch off “Backup Reminders”.

What happens when I mirror my screen?

As soon as you mirror your screen via AirPlay or cable, or record it, GroupMix automatically hides level, gender, and rules. Group cards get larger, and the display stays on while the groups are shown. There’s nothing to set up.

How do I add a class list?

In a course, tap “+” at the top. There are three ways.

Add Person

Type names one by one.

Paste Name List

  1. Copy the list, for example from an email, Excel, or Numbers.
  2. In the course, tap “+” and choose “Paste Name List”.
  3. Paste the list: one name per line, optionally with gender and level, for example Hedwig L.;f;4.
  4. Check the preview and tap the checkmark.

Import from File

  1. Prepare a CSV file with a header row, for example: 
    First Name;Last Name;Gender;Level
Hedwig;Lange;f;4
Konrad;Maurer;m;
  2. In the course, tap “+” and choose “Import from File”.
  3. Tap “Choose CSV File…” and select the file.
  4. Check the preview and tap the checkmark.

Gender: m, f, x, or empty. Level: 1 (lower) to 5 (higher); empty means medium. First and last name become “Hedwig L.”. Other columns are ignored. German column titles work too.

What does the level mean?

The level is your assessment of where a person stands compared to the rest of the course, for example their current attainment in your subject. You choose one of five steps from “Low” to “High”. It’s optional.

With the level, you can make groups in two ways:

  • Mixed Levels: Each group gets people with higher and lower attainment and is about equally strong on average.
  • Similar Levels: People with similar attainment work together, for example on tasks of different difficulty.

The level is only used for making groups. It never appears in shared images or text and is hidden while you mirror your screen.

What do I do at the end of the school year?

When a term is over, the course list shows a notice at the top.

  1. Tap “Clean Up” in the notice.
  2. Check the new term, for example 2026/27. With “Increment Names”, 8b automatically becomes 9b.
  3. Check the courses you’re carrying over and choose “Move Checked Courses”.
  4. Delete the remaining courses.

Keep personal data only as long as necessary.

Can I use GroupMix at school?

GroupMix processes names and, if you enter them, assessments of your students’ attainment. Using personal devices for school work is subject to the data protection rules of your school and region. If in doubt, ask your school’s leadership or data protection officer.
