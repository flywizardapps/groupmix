Frequently Asked Questions

Where is my data stored?

Only on your device, encrypted. GroupMix uses no cloud, no account, and no internet connection. The data isn’t included in the device’s iCloud or computer backup either. If you lose the device or delete the app, the data is gone unless you created a backup.

How do I back up my data or move it to another device?

In the course list, tap “…” at the top and choose “Create Backup”. You set a password that encrypts the file. Then save the file in Files or send it to your other device via AirDrop. There, open the file or choose “…” and then “Import Backup”.

The password isn’t stored anywhere. Without it, a backup can’t be restored. GroupMix reminds you when your last backup was a while ago.

What happens when I mirror my screen?

As soon as you mirror your screen via AirPlay or cable, or record it, GroupMix automatically hides level, gender, and rules. Group cards get larger, and the display stays on while the groups are shown.

How do I add a class list?

In a course, tap “+”. There are three ways:

  • Add Person: type names one by one.
  • Paste Name List: one name per line, optionally with gender and level, e.g. Hedwig L.;f;4. You can paste columns straight from Excel or Numbers.
  • Import from File: a CSV file with a header row, for example:
First Name;Last Name;Gender;Level
Hedwig;Lange;f;4
Konrad;Maurer;m;

Gender: m, f, x, or empty. Level: 1 (lower) to 5 (higher); empty means medium. First and last name become “Hedwig L.”. Other columns are ignored. German column titles work too.

What does the level mean?

Your own assessment of a person compared to the course, in five steps from “Low” to “High”. It’s optional. It’s only used for making groups and never appears in shared images or text.

What do I do at the end of the school year?

When a term is over, the course list shows a notice. With “Clean Up”, you move courses to the new term (8b can become 9b) and delete the rest. Keep personal data only as long as necessary.

Can I use GroupMix at school?

GroupMix processes names and, if you enter them, assessments of students. Using personal devices for school work is subject to the data protection rules of your school and region. If in doubt, ask your school’s leadership or data protection officer. How GroupMix handles data is described in the privacy policy.
