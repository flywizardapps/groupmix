In One Sentence

GroupMix sorts a class into groups in seconds, at random, by level, or as a jigsaw, and keeps all data on the device.

In One Paragraph

GroupMix is an iPhone and iPad app that lets teachers sort their class into groups in seconds. If needed, it takes level and gender into account, follows rules about who should work together or apart, and avoids repeats. It hands out roles and topics, plans jigsaw activities, and picks who presents the results. All data stays encrypted on the device. When the screen is mirrored to a projector or whiteboard, GroupMix automatically hides level, gender, and rules.

In Detail

Group work in class often starts with slips of paper, counting off, or debates. GroupMix replaces that with a single tap: mark who’s present, choose a group size, done. Groups appear as colored cards and can be shared as an image or text, for a projector, whiteboard, or learning platform.

There’s more one level down: groups with mixed or similar levels, balancing by gender, rules like “Mia and Paul work apart,” and a memory that avoids repeats. Roles from cooperative learning rotate from one grouping to the next. For a jigsaw, GroupMix creates home groups and expert groups in one step. Fixed groupings such as table groups can be saved under a name. At the end, the app draws the order of the groups and picks one person per group to present.

Because the app works with student data, it is built to stay local: no account, no cloud, no tracking, no network access. Backups are created by the user and protected with a password.

Key Features

  • Groups by number or size, at random or by level, with gender balance
  • Rules for “together” and “apart”, avoid repeats
  • Roles and topics per group, jigsaw with home and expert groups
  • Put groups together manually and save them under a name
  • Adjust results afterwards, 14-day history
  • Draw the order of groups, random pick of who presents
  • Mirroring privacy: level, gender, and rules disappear automatically when the screen is shared
  • Import from a name list or CSV file, clean up at the end of the school year

Background

GroupMix is made by Anton Bastian, a math and physics teacher in Hamburg, Germany. He wanted a tool for his own classes that makes it quick to organize meaningful cooperative learning: groups that fit by level, rules for working together, and above all roles and topics, so that everyone in a group has a job.

GroupMix grew up close to the classroom. New features were tried out in his own classes first, and colleagues used the app in everyday teaching through TestFlight. Many features come from their feedback, such as the reminder to review levels regularly or better-mixed expert groups in jigsaw activities.

“Cooperative learning works best when the lesson keeps flowing. With GroupMix, groups are ready in seconds, and roles and topics make them fit the learning goal.”

Anton Bastian, developer of GroupMix
